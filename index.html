Enterprise Architecture

Built specifically for high-performing automotive sales.

Automotive sales teams cannot risk unauthorized AI chatbots texting buyers erratically. AutoAgent isolates the AI into a strict private co-pilot mode.

📱

Sales Companion First

All customer chats stay 100% anchored to the salesperson's real device. The AI agent never converses with clients directly or sends unprompted replies.

  • ✓ Passive indexing bridge over local SQLite
  • ✓ Outbound follow-ups send from your real number
  • ✓ Drop-in Baileys companion link protocol
📑

CRM Dual-Logging Engine

Eliminates clerical diary upkeep. AutoAgent synchronizes today's 30+ diary entries, moves follow-up dates, and stamps notes into permanent customer ERA files.

  • ✓ Automated ColdFusion portal interaction
  • ✓ Permanent ERA audit trail preservation
  • ✓ Deal heat scoring based on touchpoints & quotes
⚡

Sub-10ms Stock Engine

Local inventory cache with sub-second lookups across branches. Instantly generates formatted WhatsApp vehicle cards and high-resolution photo sets.

  • ✓ Real-time price, mileage, and branch filtering
  • ✓ Automated multi-image gallery downloads
  • ✓ Daily 2:00 AM cron inventory synchronization
🛡️

Bulletproof Cultural Guard

Context-aware multi-tier language architecture. Detects South African naming conventions to ensure culturally appropriate English or Afrikaans outreach.

  • ✓ Indigenous African name safety guard (English standard)
  • ✓ Traditional Afrikaans name adaptation
  • ✓ Strict long dash ban prevents CRM text mangling
Interactive Deployment

From zero to operational in 6 steps.

The deploy script handles dependencies, Antigravity brain setup, profile variables, WhatsApp dual-pairing, and background daemonization.

01

Environment Verification

Verifies Node.js 22 LTS, Python 3, PM2, and portal scraping dependencies automatically.

02

Antigravity AI Brain

Installs and tests the Antigravity CLI (agy) runtime for agentic tool calling and reasoning.

03

Variable Capture

Prompts for salesperson name, primary number, dealership name, and optional Telegram tokens.

04

Dual QR Pairing

Scan QR 1 to pair your sales companion monitor, then scan QR 2 to activate your private co-pilot bot.

05

Portal Authentication

Tests CRM portal credentials live and downloads today's active diary entries immediately.

06

Daemon Launch

Deploys the monitor, bot, health check, and watchdog under PM2 for persistent auto-recovery.
